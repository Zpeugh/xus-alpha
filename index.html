01Mid-cycle resetLAT 00 // MARKET STRUCTURE

Why the
window is open

European defense primes — Rheinmetall, Hensoldt, Saab, Leonardo, Renk — ran 100-300% in 2025, consolidated through 2H25 on Ukraine-peace speculation, then resumed climbing into spring 2026. The STOXX Europe Aerospace & Defence Index pulled back ~11% from its 2026 peak by mid-April even as earnings estimates rose — the sector is still compounding on NATO's 3.5%-of-GDP commitment and Germany's constitutional debt-brake exemption, endorsed by all 27 EU members on 6 March 2026.

Primes cannot convert faster than their subcontractors can supply propellants, seekers, radars, datalinks and rocket motors.

Critical minerals are in a separate, non-correlated cycle driven by Chinese export controls on tungsten, antimony, gallium, germanium and heavy REEs, with Western sovereign balance sheets — DoD IBAS, EU CRMA, Canada CMS, Australia CMF — actively writing checks. The mispricings cluster on ASX, TSX-V, AIM, Warsaw and Borsa Italiana: thinner institutional coverage, more forced-seller overhangs from ETF rebalancing, and options chains that have not yet adjusted implied vols to the new policy regime.

14What changes this call// TAIL CONDITIONS

Three events, together.

The thesis breaks if three things happen simultaneously. Any one in isolation is survivable by the basket; all three together would require cutting gross exposure.

// THE TRIPLE LOCK
01

A genuine Russia-Ukraine settlement that the market believes sticks.

02

A meaningful US-China critical-minerals detente lifting Chinese export controls on tungsten, antimony, gallium and germanium.

03

A hawkish ECB / Fed pivot that compresses small-cap mining multiples.

SENTINEL 01

NATO June 2026 communiqué

The "3.5+1.5" commitment has to translate into actual national-budget lines. Watch the summit language carefully for binding floors versus aspirational ceilings.

SENTINEL 02

H2 2026 Chinese export-control amendments

The next scheduled round. Loosening would compress the tungsten and antimony window; tightening the opposite.

SENTINEL 03

Advanced-reactor HALEU signings

X-energy, TerraPower, Oklo, Westinghouse eVinci. Any signed HALEU offtake with GLE re-prices SLX immediately.

15Position sizing framework// PORTFOLIO-LEVEL DISCIPLINE

The conviction is the portfolio,
not the name.

// 4 liquid US options · 10 overseas microcap anchors
RULE 01 · OPTIONALITY

Four names carry liquid US options.

IperionX, Almonty, and via ADRs Hensoldt and Saab allow defined-risk expression. Use options to leverage after underlying runs; do not initiate options on illiquid chains.

RULE 02 · SIZING

ASX and TSX-V microcaps at 1-3% notional each.

Hard mental stop at the project-financing catalyst. Conviction comes from the portfolio, not from any single idea surviving a left-tail macro event.

RULE 03 · DRAWDOWN

Accept 30-40% drawdowns on individual names.

In rearmament and re-onshoring cycles, the best risk-adjusted outcome comes from accepting volatility in exchange for the 100-200% upside that policy-locked demand creates in bottleneck suppliers.

// Closing note

Policy-locked demand, physical supply that cannot respond, small enough to re-rate violently.

The setup is the confluence — not any single contract, any single name, or any single catalyst.
